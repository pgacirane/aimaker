Our Foundation

Mission & Vision

We exist to provide rigorous, evidence-based regulatory advisory services that enable clients to engage confidently with RURA and the legal instruments governing Rwanda's ICT sector.

Mission

Bridging the Regulatory Gap

Regulatory Advisor exists to bridge the gap between Rwanda's evolving ICT regulatory architecture and the operators, investors, and institutions that must navigate it. Our mission is to provide rigorous, evidence-based regulatory advisory services that enable clients to engage confidently with the Rwanda Utilities Regulatory Authority (RURA) and the legal instruments governing Rwanda's ICT sector.

Vision

The Reference Firm for ICT Counsel

To be the reference firm for ICT regulatory counsel in Rwanda — one that policymakers, licensed operators, and market entrants turn to when precision, legal grounding, and sector fluency are non-negotiable. We believe that a well-regulated ICT sector is foundational to Rwanda's socio-economic development.

What We Do

Core Services

Comprehensive ICT regulatory advisory spanning spectrum, standards, postal services, media, and regulatory engagement across Rwanda's multi-layered framework.

01

Spectrum & Numbering Resource Advisory

Navigate Rwanda's Frequency Allocation Table, spectrum licensing procedures, and the Rwanda National Numbering Plan. We guide applications for operators including MTN, Airtel, KTRN, and Liquid Telecom frameworks.

02

ICT Standards, Type Approval & QoS Compliance

Assist with type approval of terminal equipment, infrastructure roll-out aligned with RURA guidelines, e-waste management obligations, and cybersecurity compliance requirements.

03

Postal, Courier & E-Commerce Licensing

Support companies seeking initial licensing, renewal, or compliance review under RURA's postal and courier mandate. Advise e-commerce platforms on cross-border online trade regulatory frameworks.

04

Media Licensing & Broadcasting Compliance

Guide media operators through RURA authorization for radio, television, and internet broadcasting. Advise on content standards, consumer protection, and restrictions on monopolistic conduct.

05

Regulatory Engagement & Representation

Prepare formal submissions, regulatory filings, and compliance documentation for RURA across all ICT sub-sectors. Structured preparation for licensing hearings and regulatory inquiries.

06

Ongoing Regulatory Monitoring

Monitor changes to regulatory instruments, RURA Board Decisions, sector reports and strategic plans — ensuring clients remain current with Rwanda's ICT regulatory direction of travel.

Why Engage Us

Value Proposition

Rwanda's ICT regulatory environment is multi-layered, spanning telecommunications, spectrum, postal services, e-commerce, media, cybersecurity, and standards compliance — each governed by distinct legal instruments and administered by RURA's specialised units.

For operators and investors without in-house regulatory capacity, this complexity carries material risk: non-compliance with SIM card registration regulations, failure to meet quality of service standards, or an incomplete licensing submission can result in operational disruption or market exclusion.

Regulatory Advisor converts that complexity into manageable, actionable steps. Our value lies not in generic legal commentary, but in sector-specific regulatory intelligence grounded in RURA's published instruments and the practical knowledge of how Rwanda's regulatory processes function.
